For small archive, HPC and media teams that run tape libraries without a vendor engineer on site. Nemotron reads the drive and library telemetry and drafts what to do; a deterministic validator can veto any claim the readings do not back. Hero readings are real tape-tool output (sg_logs, mtx, sg_turs, LTFS) from mhVTL, an open-source virtual tape library.

          Provenance and limitations

          This incident, selected model: · ·

          The hero readings come from mhVTL, an open-source virtual tape library, with faults injected on purpose; it is not real drive hardware or a real site. The other incidents are hand-written by the same author as the runbooks. No operator study or time saving is measured. A cache record is local evidence, not cryptographic attestation by the provider. The validator checks that a claim is backed by the readings; it cannot see context the readings do not encode, which is why a stronger reader is required before any physical action.
