              The hero readings come from mhVTL, an open-source virtual tape library, with faults injected on purpose; it is not real drive hardware or a real site. The other incidents are hand-written by the same author as the runbooks. No operator study or time saving is measured. A cache record is local evidence, not cryptographic attestation by the provider.
